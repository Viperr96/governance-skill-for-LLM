## Reply shape

- Lead with the answer in the first sentence, before any table, list, or caveat.
- Carry status, comparisons, and any multi-item result in a table or a bullet list, never a prose paragraph.
- Cap unbroken prose at two paragraphs; if a third starts, convert the run to a bullet list or a table.
- Give each item its own bullet row, not a clause buried across sentences.
- Compose in this shape from the start; do not draft prose and reshape it afterward.
- A short confirmation (`Done.`, `Yes, that works.`) is already the answer; leave it as one line.
